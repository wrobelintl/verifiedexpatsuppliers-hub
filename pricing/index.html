What is included

  • One listing on the relevant country guide.
  • Identity verification against the official public register for your country, with the date shown on your card.
  • Your own contact route published — enquiries go directly to you. We take no commission and no per-lead fee.
  • Annual re-verification at renewal.

Not included — and not for sale at any price

  • Ranking or listing position.
  • A "featured" or premium tier.
  • Any rating, score or star display. None exists on this site.
  • Removal or softening of the paid-placement disclosure.

What you are buying, stated plainly. A verified listing is identity verification only — we confirm your business exists and your details match the official public register. It is not an endorsement of your work, and your card says so.

We cannot promise you enquiries, and we do not publish figures suggesting otherwise. If that is what you are looking for, this is the wrong directory.

Billing

Invoiced annually by bank transfer. No card is stored, there is no auto-renewal, and no payment processor sits between us. If your registration lapses or no longer verifies at renewal, the listing comes down rather than being renewed.

Removal on request is refunded pro-rata for the unexpired period.
