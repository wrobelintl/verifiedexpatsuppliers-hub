What happens after you send it

  1. We reply to every application, including the ones we decline. Usually within a few working days.
  2. We run the check against the register named above and record the result with the date and the source URL.
  3. One of three outcomes. Confirmed — found, and the details match. Partial — some items confirmed, others not; we tell you which, and a partial result is disclosed on the listing. Unconfirmed — we could not find you or the details did not match, and there is no listing.
  4. You approve the wording before anything goes live.
  5. You pay after that, not before. There is no charge for applying and no charge if we decline.

Why we might decline

  • The business cannot be found on a public register we can check.
  • The description makes claims we cannot verify, and you would rather not change them.
  • Your model involves paying or receiving referral commissions — that conflicts with the no-commission position the whole directory rests on.
  • The work described is something we would not want to send a reader toward.

A decline is not a judgement on your business. Most often it just means the register does not show what we need it to show.
