Two things that catch foreign buyers out

The valor de referencia can be higher than what you paid

Since 2022 the tax base is the higher of the declared price and the cadastral reference value. If the reference value is above your price, you are taxed on the reference value. Your lawyer can check it before you sign, and it can be appealed — but not comfortably, after the fact.

New builds are not cheaper, just taxed differently

A new build pays 10% IVA plus AJD stamp duty of roughly 0.75%–1.5% depending on region, instead of ITP. In a low-ITP region like Madrid that makes a new build noticeably more expensive in tax; in Catalunya or the Balearics it can be the other way round.

What else is in the cost stack

Budget 10–15% on top of the price in total. Beyond the transfer tax that means the notary (€600–€1,200, set by state tariff), the Land Registry (€400–€1,000+), and an independent lawyer at around 1% plus 21% IVA. Read the full Spain guide for the annual costs that follow — particularly Modelo 210, the non-resident tax you owe even when the property sits empty.
