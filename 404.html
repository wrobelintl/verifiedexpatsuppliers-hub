What verified means here. We confirm that a business exists and that its details match the official public register for its profession — identity verification only. It is never an endorsement of the quality of anyone's work, and every listing card says so. We do not rate, rank or endorse anyone, we hold no review data and will never display a rating, and we take no commission or referral fee on any work that results. Listings are paid placements and are disclosed as such on the card.

Nothing here is legal, tax or immigration advice. The guides and calculators are general information. Rules and rates change, often annually. Confirm anything that will cost you money with the official authority or a qualified professional before you commit.
