What verified means here, and what it does not

This is a supplier directory with free country guides alongside it. We verify identity against official public registers; we do not rate, rank or endorse anyone, we are not affiliated with any firm or professional, and we take no commission or referral fee on any work that results.

Every country page reserves a section for verified suppliers. All four currently read "applications open — no suppliers listed yet", and that is accurate: there are none yet. Where a supplier does appear, "verified" means we hold a dated record of their registration or licence number checked against the issuing register, a confirmed address and contact route, and their written agreement to be listed. Nothing appears before that evidence exists, and we will never display a rating or review score, because we have no review data and will not invent any.

Nothing here is legal, tax or immigration advice. Rules and rates change, often annually. Confirm anything that will cost you money with the official authority or a qualified professional.
