How this works

Why an empty directory is the honest starting point

01

We check identity, not quality

We confirm the business exists and its details match the official register for its profession — a bar roll for a lawyer, a professional college for a clinician, the commercial register for a company. We have not used their services and cannot judge whether they are good.

02

Enquiries go straight to them

Your listing shows your contact route and the reader contacts you directly. We do not collect the enquiry, we do not forward it, we never see it, and we take no commission on any work that results.

03

Nothing appears before the evidence does

All four country pages currently read “applications open — no suppliers listed yet”, and that is accurate. We would rather show an empty section than a name we cannot evidence.
