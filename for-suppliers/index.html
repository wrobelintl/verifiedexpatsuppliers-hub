Get listed in the verified supplier directory

For firms and practices serving expats in Spain, Portugal, Mexico and Costa Rica. Exactly what we check before you appear, and the things we will not do at any price.

Read this first: a verified listing is identity verification only.

It means we have confirmed that your business exists and that the details you gave us match official public records. It is never an endorsement of the quality of your work. We have not used your services, we have not spoken to your clients, and we are not in a position to judge whether you are good at your job.

Every listing on this site says so, in those words, on the card itself. If you are looking for a directory that will call you "top rated" or "trusted", this is not that directory and never will be.

Categories we list

The directory covers the services an expat actually has to buy in their first two years abroad. Applications are open in all seven categories:

Accounting & taxAsesores fiscales, contabilistas, gestores, cross-border tax advisersThe commercial register for the firm, plus the relevant professional body register where the profession is regulated

Where a profession is not regulated, we do not pretend otherwise. A relocation consultant cannot be checked against a licensing body because none exists — so their card says the company was confirmed and nothing more. We would rather show you a smaller claim than a borrowed one.

Which register, country by country

Which register we use depends on your profession as well as your country — a lawyer is checked against the bar roll, a company against the commercial register, a clinician against their professional college. This is the country-level checklist. Each item is checked against a public register, and we record the source URL and the date we checked it. If something cannot be confirmed, it stays unconfirmed — we never upgrade a result by inference or because an applicant assures us it is fine.

CountryWhat we check it against
🇪🇸 Spain Company registration via the Registro Mercantil, or for lawyers the relevant Colegio de Abogados register. Plus: NIF, an active website whose identity matches the claim, and a business address.
🇵🇹 Portugal NIPC via the registo comercial, or for lawyers the Ordem dos Advogados roll. Plus: an active website whose identity matches the claim, and a business address.
🇲🇽 Mexico RFC registration, or for notaries the relevant Colegio de Notarios. Plus: an active website whose identity matches the claim, and a business address.
🇨🇷 Costa Rica Cédula jurídica via the Registro Nacional, or for lawyers the Colegio de Abogados y Abogadas. Plus: an active website whose identity matches the claim, and a business address.

Each check produces one of three outcomes, and we publish the date it was carried out:

  • Confirmed — found in the register, and the details match what you told us.
  • Partial — some items confirmed, others not. We will tell you which, and a partial result is disclosed on the listing.
  • Unconfirmed — we could not find you, or the details did not match. No listing.

What we need from you

  1. Written consent to be listed. We list nobody who has not asked. A reply by email saying you want to be listed is enough, and we keep it on file.
  2. The registration number for your country from the table above.
  3. A contact route we can publish — the email, phone or page you want enquiries to arrive at.
  4. Agreement to our corrections policy: if a reader tells us something on your listing is wrong, we will ask you about it and correct it. You can have your listing removed at any time, for any reason, without notice or argument.

Enquiries go straight to you

We do not sit in the middle. Your listing shows your contact route, and a reader who wants to speak to you contacts you directly. We do not collect the enquiry, we do not forward it, we do not see it, and we take no commission on any work that results. There is nothing for us to intercept and no per-lead fee.

What we will never do

  • Sell a rating or a ranking. Paying more will never buy a better position or a better score.
  • Invent reviews or testimonials. Not now, not to fill space, not ever.
  • List anyone who has not asked to be listed. This site previously carried example entries that were not real businesses; they were deleted, and that will not happen again.
  • Display stars. There is no rating display anywhere on a listing card. If client reviews are introduced later they will be real, moderated, and shown with the number they are based on — "3 reviews" and the reviews themselves, never a star average implying a body of opinion that does not exist.

Paid placement — stated plainly

Listings are paid placements. Suppliers pay a fee to appear on this site, and every listing card carries this disclosure:

Listed supplier — paid placement, identity verified on <date>

That disclosure is on the card itself, next to the business name, not buried in a footer or a terms page. This is a legal requirement under FTC rules on endorsements and disclosure, and equivalent consumer-protection norms in the EU, and not something we would remove for a fee.

Paying does not affect what we verify, the outcome we record, or the order listings appear in. If your identity check fails, the fee is not a route around it.
