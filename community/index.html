How to find one now, without us

You need this today, not whenever this page fills up. These are the routes that actually work, and none of them involve us.

English-language and international churches

  • Anglican / Church of England. Continental Europe is covered by the Diocese in Europe, which runs chaplaincies in Spain and Portugal, including in most areas with a large British population. Their site lists congregations by country.
  • Catholic. Find the local diocese for your town and look for the parish directory. In coastal Spain and the Algarve many parishes run a Mass in English, usually weekly; the parish office is the fastest way to confirm times, which change seasonally.
  • Evangelical, international and non-denominational. These rarely appear in national directories. Searching the town name with “international church” or “iglesia evangélica” finds more than any aggregator, and expat Facebook groups for your specific town will answer this in under an hour.
  • Jewish, Muslim and other faiths. Community organisations tend to be city-level and are best found through the national federation for that faith rather than a local search.

Secular community groups

  • Your town hall’s register of associations. The most under-used resource of the lot. Spanish ayuntamientos and Portuguese câmaras municipais keep a public registro de asociaciones covering sports clubs, choirs, hiking groups, language exchanges and neighbourhood associations. Ask at the front desk, or search the town hall site.
  • Meetup for language exchanges, hiking, board games and running clubs — strongest in bigger cities.
  • InterNations for organised expat events. Membership-gated and more corporate in feel, but the events are real and regular.
  • Language exchange (intercambio) evenings. Usually free, usually in a bar, and the single fastest way to meet both locals and other new arrivals. Bars and language schools advertise them locally rather than online.

The advice nobody gives you: join something with a fixed weekly time before you have unpacked. A commitment that recurs beats an intention that does not, and the group matters less than the recurrence. A bad choir on Thursdays will do more for you than a perfect one you never get round to.
